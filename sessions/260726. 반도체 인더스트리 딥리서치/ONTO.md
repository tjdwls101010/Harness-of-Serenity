# ONTO Innovation — the qualification gate on new package architectures

**TLDR:** The one current holding I'd actually add to. Inspection is a gate, not a design slot — a new packaging architecture cannot ramp until someone qualifies an inspection recipe for it — and the consensus gap is real at roughly +40% with zero insider selling. Not inflecting violently, so it's a Tier 2 you build patiently rather than a Tier 1 you chase.

## Structural position (Bottleneck)

Onto sits in advanced-package and panel-level inspection/metrology. The mechanism that makes this a bottleneck rather than a cost line: every new package architecture — CoWoS variants, panel-level fan-out, glass-core — needs a qualified inspection recipe before volume, so the tool gates the ramp. The independent map calls advanced-package inspection *"one of the strongest complexity-tax layers"* and I agree with that framing more than my own first pass, which over-tagged the broader packaging category.

Second role, tagged **DEDUCED**: Onto is named in the Serenity thesis DB as the metrology partner for LPKF's LIDE glass-core process. Not verified against a filing (EDGAR was 403 all session). It is a free option on glass-core, not part of the base case.

Net effect: ONTO's position as the best add among current holdings is reinforced, and the tier stays 2 only because the *stage* is still 3 — steady 21.7% growth rather than a violent inflection. If growth accelerates while packaging share keeps climbing, this becomes a Tier 1.
