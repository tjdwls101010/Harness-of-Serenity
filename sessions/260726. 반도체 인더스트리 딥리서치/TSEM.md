# TSEM — clean inflection, price already there

**TLDR:** Tier 1 on structure and the cleanest insider record in the whole cohort — one sale in twelve months while peers' insiders dumped hundreds of millions. Margins are expanding steadily on rising revenue. But the lens puts it at almost exactly PEG-fair on two-year consensus, so the right call is **hold, stop adding, re-add under ~$131**.

## Structural position (Bottleneck — contested)

Tower is a specialty/mature-node foundry: silicon photonics, SiGe/RF, BCD power, MEMS. The layer matters because photonic integrated circuits need a mature-node process with photonics-specific device libraries and qualification — leading-edge logic fabs are neither cheap nor suitable, so the capacity sits in a short specialty tier. Every silicon-photonics and CPO story on the map runs through a fab in this layer.

**The disagreement, recorded rather than smoothed:** my map tagged this a bottleneck; the independent map argues a customer-owned PIC can be ported after redesign and requalification, making it a capital-and-time constraint, and that unused capacity captures nothing. I think that read has real force and it is unresolved. The falsifier is clean: **a volume customer publicly stating that a proprietary Tower process module is sole-source and cannot be ported within the product window.** Absent that, treat the bottleneck tag as provisional.

EDGAR was 403 all session, but a research agent sourced these from company press releases, which is a legitimate CONFIRMED channel. This is the most consequential new evidence of the whole session, and it lands on a name you hold.

**Named customers — CONFIRMED (press release, not inference):**

**The contracted book — CONFIRMED, degree disclosed, names withheld (PR 2026-05-13):**
- **$1.3B of 2027 silicon-photonics revenue already under contract**
- **$290M of customer prepayments for capacity reservation**
- 2028 described as "substantially larger," with further prepayments due January 2027

Put that against TTM revenue of $1,622M. A single product line has more than 80% of the company's *current total* revenue contracted for 2027 — and customers have wired **$290M of their own cash** to reserve capacity.

**Why the prepayment is the fact that matters.** Nobody prepays to reserve a fungible process. Third-party capital at risk, on contract terms, is exactly the discriminator the funded-vs-dilution gate asks for, and Tower clears it — not with a partnership headline but with cash on the balance sheet from the counterparties. It is also *revealed unportability*: the strongest available evidence that these flows cannot simply be moved to another foundry inside the product window.

**The sole-source question — still honestly NO.** No explicit sole-source or unportable language exists in any Tower or customer disclosure. And there is real counter-evidence: when Intel abandoned the New Mexico corridor, Tower **redirected those customers back to Fab 7 in Japan** — which proves portability *within Tower's own network*, not across foundries, so it does not settle the question either way. My read stands as **capital-and-time constraint with bottleneck-like pricing today** (multi-year requalification), not a proven physical chokepoint. The archetype tag stays provisional.

**Correction to a widely-repeated capacity fact:** the **Intel New Mexico 300mm corridor is dead or in mediation** — Tower disclosed on 2026-02-11 that "Intel has expressed its intention not to perform under the agreement. The parties are presently in a mediation process." Most published TSEM capacity tables still credit that corridor. They are wrong. Current silicon-photonics volume runs on **two 200mm US fabs** (Newport Beach CA, San Antonio TX); the 300mm growth path is Japan — Uozu Fab 7 full ownership closing 2027-04-01 (quadrupling capacity) plus a $3B Arai expansion net of $1B METI grants (2026-07-14).
