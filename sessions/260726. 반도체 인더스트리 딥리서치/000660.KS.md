# SK Hynix — the HBM leader, priced with more skepticism than its peers

**TLDR:** Tier 1. Revenue tripled in five quarters, operating margin went 42.2% → 71.5%, all six winner gates pass, and it is **−41.1% from its 52-week high**. The no-growth floor computed on current margins sits **54% ABOVE** the spot price — meaning the market has already discounted a large margin normalisation. That is the opposite of what I found at SNDK, and it forces a correction to my own memory call.

## Structural position (Bottleneck)

The HBM share leader. HBM is a genuine physical bottleneck: it consumes materially more wafer per bit than commodity DRAM, each stack adds TSV and bonding steps whose yield falls with height, and adding capacity is a fab construction cycle rather than a line reconfiguration. Capital cannot fix it inside the demand window.

Sourced position facts: **2026 output fully sold out and heavily committed into 2027**, with the CEO publicly flagging 2027 as the worst-ever HBM supply crunch. The industry shortfall is *widening* — roughly 5% (2025) → 6% (2026) → 9% (2027), with no supply/demand balance projected until 2Q28.

One hop down is the thing to watch: **HBM4/4E base dies move to TSMC N12/N3P**, where HBM3E used in-house base dies. Three rival memory makers now converge on one foundry for the differentiating layer. SK Hynix owns the stack; it does not own that hop.

## Valuation — lens run, both legs, and one leg deliberately refused

`Lens: SKH no-growth floor — 52.58조 × 4 × 0.7153 × 0.85 ÷ 709.85M × 15 = ₩2,702,073` → **+53.6% vs ₩1,759,000**

That is the headline. Capitalising *current* margins at a no-growth 15× lands **above** the spot price. Restated the blunt way: the stock trades at roughly **9.8× annualised current-quarter net income**. The market is not paying for permanence here — it is already pricing a substantial margin decline.

`Lens: SKH PEG-fair upside — 451,026 × 40.0 = ₩18,041,040` → **+926% — REFUSED, the lens breaks**

A PEG-1 multiple on a cyclical at peak margins is not a valuation, it is an artifact. The two-year consensus EPS of ₩451,026 extrapolates peak-cycle earnings, and multiplying that by a growth-derived multiple compounds the error. Per R5, an absurd verdict is a cue to re-examine the lens, never a licence for the trade. **Substituting a mid-cycle normalisation instead:**

`Lens: SKH mid-cycle normalised — 52.58조 × 4 × 0.25 × 0.85 ÷ 709.85M × 10 = ₩629,720` → **−64% vs spot**

So the honest fork for a memory cyclical is **−64% (margins normalise to a mid-cycle 25% at a 10× memory multiple) to +54% (current margins hold, capitalised at 15×)**. Wide, which is what a cyclical at a margin extreme genuinely looks like. Consensus sits between them at ₩3,408,502 mean, which is above *both* my legs and which I do not adopt.

## Gates — all six

1. **Monetization — pass.** 71.5% operating margin, ₩24.38조 real FCF.
2. **Pricing realization — pass, emphatically.** 29 points of operating margin in five quarters is realised, not theoretical.
3. **Survival — pass.** Net debt ₩9.83조 against a ₩1,248.6조 cap is a rounding error.
4. **TAM expansion — pass.** HBM stack count and capacity per accelerator both rising.
5. **Allocation control — pass.** This is the strongest gate here: as the share leader on a sold-out book, it *is* the allocation.
6. **Demand breadth — pass.** Sells to every accelerator programme, not one.

**The conditional:** cycle-stage. Margins are at an extreme and the sourced pricing evidence is decelerating (3Q26 conventional DRAM +13–18% QoQ after an +81% QoQ industry quarter; NAND +10–15% at "a noticeably slower pace"). From 3Q26, US CSP multi-year LTAs also **contractually cap** realised pricing. That caps the tier at "Tier 1 with a named condition," not an unqualified one.

## Cycle stage — 4, not 5

Revenue is still accelerating (32.83 → 52.58) and margins still expanding. Stage 5 requires deceleration or a dated cliff, and neither is in this name's own evidence. The cycle risk is prospective, not realised — which is exactly why it is a *conditional gate* rather than a stage demotion.

## Downsides

- **Peak margins.** A 71.5% operating margin in memory is a shortage, not a business quality. *Partly priced in* — that is what the 9.8× and the −41% drawdown are telling you.
- **LTA price caps.** The contracts that de-risk 2026–27 revenue cap the upside pricing. *Not priced in.*
- **2027–28 supply.** SK Hynix's own internal model reportedly contemplates 2027 oversupply, and roughly $518B of Korean capacity lands 2027–28. *Not priced in* — the real bear case.
- **Base-die dependency.** HBM4E base dies move to TSMC; the differentiating layer is one hop outside the company. *Not priced in.*
- **FX cuts favourably here, and it is worth being precise.** Revenue is dollar-denominated, costs are largely KRW — so a **weak won is margin-accretive**. For a KRW-denominated holder that is a clean tailwind, not the translation drag it would be through a US-listed ETF wrapper.
- **Vehicle.** No IV data on the KRX line in this pipeline, so the cash-secured-put ladder used elsewhere in this cohort is unavailable. Shares only. The SKHY ADS is the same underlying and adds **zero diversification** — with a 1-ADS-equals-1/10-share sizing trap.

## Rating

**Tier 1, conditional on cycle-stage — and the best-priced expression inside memory.** Gates all pass, stage 4, floor above spot, zero insider selling, and it is the share leader on a sold-out book. Against MU it is slightly more expensive on peak earnings (9.8× vs 9.2×) but more drawn down (−41.1% vs −26.6%) and holds the HBM share lead; those roughly offset, and I would not switch between them on this evidence.

Given the position is already held: **hold, and add on further weakness rather than at spot.** The regime dial for this cohort is constructive-but-staged, and a name whose bear leg is −64% deserves the staging.
